§1 Profile

I enjoy working across the full stack to ship things that actually work. My practice centers on business platforms — inventory, ERP, e-commerce — designed for reliability first: offline-first architectures, cloud synchronization, and infrastructure I continue to maintain long after launch.

“Design it, ship it, then keep the servers running.”

Four enterprise systems anchor the practice: an auto-parts management platform on a secure internal network, a cloud enterprise suite for industrial supply, a contracting and CRM platform, and an offline-first pharmacy network with loyalty and POS.

Alongside client work, three products ship under my own hand — two generations of a retail ERP called Rocket, and Egyfy, a multi-tenant SaaS for branded storefronts, currently in beta with three live tenants — plus three open-source tools for JVM developers and beyond.
